Background

Beyond the Resume

What pulled me into DevOps was the architecture. The first time I saw a commit trigger a build, run tests, package a container, and ship to production with no human touching it, I wanted to build more of that.

Then Kubernetes clicked. The scheduling, self-healing, rollbacks, resource allocation. It's a genuinely interesting system to run in production when you know what's happening under the hood.

My MS in Computer Engineering gave me the foundation. Production systems gave me the rest. There's a version of engineering you learn in school, and a different one you learn when rollbacks have to work at 2am.

I care about the rollback as much as the deploy. Anyone can ship. The interesting part is getting back to a good state, fast, without a runbook that's six months out of date.
